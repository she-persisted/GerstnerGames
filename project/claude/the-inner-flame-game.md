# The Inner Flame (Child I's game)

Live game: https://claude.ai/artifact/FAgoyVHV7dqGaY856XeSk6
Source: GitHub `she-persisted/GerstnerGames`, `library/games/inner-flame/legacy/v1.html` (one file, kept unchanged; see claude/game-library.md)

Concept (designed by Child I, with Mom): a tall, scared robot who starts out alone travels through space to find the flame that can rebuild his dark home world, Ashlund. He solves riddles and brain teasers, and makes friends who help him along the way. Built for Child I's Chromebook, with typing practice throughout.

## How it plays
- Player names the robot, then names the magic Bernedoodle when they meet.
- Flights between planets are typing drills (type each word to fire the thrusters; letters turn green or pink).
- Planets: Puffwhirl (classic riddles, meet the magic Bernedoodle), Frostmere (word scrambles, meet Pebble the space penguin), Mapstone (Earth and continent questions plus a number pattern, meet Atlas the mossy rock), Ember Nebula (space riddles on a three-floor tower, then type "I am brave and I am not alone" to take the flame).
- Helpers: Scan letters (robot), dog sniffs a clue, Pebble peeks a letter, Atlas ponders a second clue. After 3 wrong tries, "Show me the answer" appears and he types it to continue.
- Courage meter rises with each answer and friend; the robot trembles less as it grows.
- End screen shows words typed, typing accuracy, words per minute, puzzles solved, and clues used. Riddles are picked at random each game.
- Progress saves in the browser, so he can stop and pick up later.
